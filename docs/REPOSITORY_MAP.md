## Versioned records

Source, inputs, native CAD, solver logs and numerical outputs stay together within their captured study packages. Published scientific binaries and original captures retain their exact identities. Superseded concept documents are routed through the archive index. Active overview pages link to current and historical material without relabelling their results.

The index is checked against the Git publication set. Its counts describe stored files, not engineering completion.
