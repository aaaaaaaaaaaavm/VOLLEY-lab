# My programme transfer ledger

I use this file to stop three different actions being called “transfer”:

1. A result stays in the repository where I produced it.
2. A reusable hypothesis may be restated here with a link and a new entry criterion.
3. Only a passing, predeclared lab gate may create a proposal for either authoritative record.

No lab result inherits the evidence class of its source. Geometry borrowed from a passing BOLLEY
field model is still an unrun VOLLEY trim hypothesis when I change its gap, pitch, depth or force
duty.

## Promotion rule

An entry may leave the lab only when all five statements are true:

1. I wrote its acceptance bands before its executable model.
2. It deletes or closes a controlling defect rather than merely improving a secondary number.
3. Installed mass, stored energy, failure concentration and interface ownership are compared on
   the same boundary as the destination baseline.
4. A failed result remains here with the number that stopped it.
5. Promotion creates a new ADR in the destination; it does not rewrite the source history.

## Things I will not transfer

- The 0.0253 CDS-rail edge factor is a rejection, not a low-confidence estimate to retry.
- The 66.7:1 Fluxfoil winding-window failure is not repaired by drawing the same coil more neatly.
- Gen5 sled optimisations do not return while Gen6 has no sled.
- A provider-dependent stage credit does not become hardware mass saved until a named host accepts
  the shared structure.
- “No restraints” does not relax conservation, structural load paths, contamination control,
  launch safety or evidence provenance.
