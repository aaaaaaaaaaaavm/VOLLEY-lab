# BOLLEY branches I quarantine here

These ideas originate in BOLLEY's unknown-unknown search. I keep the authoritative problem and
result, where one exists, in BOLLEY. This register prevents an attractive branch becoming an
unannounced baseline change.

| Candidate | What it may buy | Current stop or entry condition |
|---|---|---|
| Null-flux differential trim | Passive lateral restoring force and centring | Needs a geometry and force map that preserve spacecraft passivity and the interface mass boundary |
| Shunted permanent-magnet secondary | Lower slip and pulse loss | Violates the no-permanent-magnet premise; needs a magnetic-cleanliness and net-system comparison before any CAD |
| Thomson-coil repulsion | No long moving launcher member | Recreates the pulse and EMI defects BOLLEY P41 is trying to delete; held unless a source closes them on installed mass |
| Digitally selected spring bank | Energy that can wait without a pulse inverter | Needs a resettable pusher, latch, safing and twelve-shot mass ledger that beats the gas route |
| Strainrail | Store deployment energy in the cooperative structure | Failed BOLLEY's first 350 J/kg screen at 749.9 J/kg; closed unless material/geometry evidence changes that bound |
| Centrifugal carousel | Mechanical bulk velocity with electromagnetic trim | Host momentum, packaging and rotating containment need a mission that values them rather than a generic deployer |
| Burn-and-drop | Relative velocity without a high-energy ejector | Needs a named host, propellant, burn arc, operations authority and release-safety comparison; it is a mission branch |
| Unmodified four-finger fallback | Retain some four-face authority without changing the spacecraft | BOLLEY P17; needs its own requirements and one-corner actuator screen |
| Standardised Fluxsleeve | Use BOLLEY's passive secondary on a launcher-owned carrier | Needs to beat PII-18 on mass, retention, arrest and payload-class coverage |
| Vectoring muzzle | Command lateral as well as axial departure velocity | Needs a reachable-domain benefit large enough to justify lateral structure and sensing |
| Opposed paired release | Cancel host recoil and campaign attitude accumulation | Needs a manifest whose orbital objectives tolerate paired opposite impulses and a safe dual-release fault case |

An entry leaves this table only when it receives its own file, source boundary and predeclared
gate. Novelty is not a gate.
