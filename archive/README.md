# Research archive

[Complete catalogue](../README.md#complete-research-catalogue) · [Archived concept documents](concepts/) · [Retained computational packages](../experiments/) · [Repository map](../docs/REPOSITORY_MAP.md)

The concept archive preserves PII architecture alternatives, VLAB investigations and historical routing records. Numerical packages remain under experiments/ with their native geometry, source, logs and result manifests together. Selection, supersession or an adverse result does not remove a study from the catalogue. Cross-project records remain outside current VOLLEY requirements.
